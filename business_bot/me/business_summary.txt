Cedar Crumb is a fictional bread bakery in Hamra, Beirut. Make slow-fermented, small-batch bread a dependable part of the everyday table. A bread-first menu with clear flour blends, useful loaf sizes, and a small rotation of Levantine-inspired sourdough flavors.

Menu (USD per loaf, approximate baked weights): Country Sourdough: 800 g, $6; Whole Wheat Sourdough: 750 g, $6; Seeded Rye: 700 g, $7; Olive & Rosemary Sourdough: 800 g, $8; Zaatar & Sesame Sourdough: 800 g, $7; Walnut & Cranberry Sourdough: 800 g, $9.

Whole Wheat Sourdough uses 70% whole-wheat and 30% white wheat flour. Seeded Rye uses 60% rye and 40% wheat flour. Sourdough batches usually ferment for 24-36 hours. Monday-Saturday, 7:00 AM-7:00 PM Beirut time. Closed Sunday. Walk-in sales, pickup preorders, weekly bread-box inquiries, and small wholesale bread inquiries are offered. 18 Cedar Lane, Hamra, Beirut (fictional address). Email: hello@cedarcrumb.example.

Team: Maya Haddad, Co-founder and head baker, handles starter care, dough development, fermentation, and daily baking. Karim Nasser, Co-founder and customer coordinator, handles pickup inquiries, weekly bread boxes, and small wholesale requests.

Pickup inquiries should arrive at least 24 hours ahead; bread-box and wholesale inquiries at least 48 hours ahead. An inquiry records interest for staff follow-up; it does not confirm an order, stock, pickup slot, subscription, discount, or payment. Flavored loaves rotate by baking day. No live stock or guaranteed daily flavor schedule is available to the assistant. All loaves contain gluten. The kitchen handles wheat, rye, sesame, walnuts, other nuts, milk, and eggs. Cross-contact is possible; no gluten-free, nut-free, sesame-free, or other allergen-free guarantee. Store bread wrapped in a clean cloth or paper at room temperature for up to two days; slice and freeze for longer storage.
